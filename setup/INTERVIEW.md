# Interview — suggested wording

> For the installer (Claude) running Phase A of `CLAUDE.md`. Ask in the author's
> language, one at a time, warmly. Adapt — don't read like a form. If they answer
> vaguely, pick a sensible default and tell them what you chose.

1. **"What kind of writing do you want help with?"**
   (papers, a thesis, grant/funding proposals, essays…) — and roughly what field.

2. **"What language will the final paper be written in?"**
   (Note this may differ from the language we're talking in right now.)

3. **"Do you already know where you want to submit?"**
   (a journal, a conference, a funder — or "not sure yet," which is fine.)

4. **"Do you have a few pieces of writing you did yourself that I could learn your
   voice from?"** (2–4 is ideal; must be *your own* prose, not AI-written.
   Optional — if not, I'll aim for clear, strong academic writing instead of
   trying to sound like you.)

5. **"How much do you want to set up today?"**
   - *"Simple — just you and me and the web, nothing to install"* (recommended to start).
   - *"The full toolkit"* — extra local tools for citations, stats, and language
     checking. (Mention it exists; don't push it. They can add it later.)

6. *(Only if they write Chinese for a Taiwan audience)* **"Do you want the
   Traditional-Chinese-Taiwan language pack?"** (Taiwan-vs-mainland term checking,
   official-terminology checking, Chinese AI-tic detection.)

After the interview, tell them what you're about to set up and where, in one or two
plain sentences, then do Phase B/C.
